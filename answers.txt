(a)
Pros: Teams can respond to the customer needs and changes in the marketplace quickly.
      Teams can get feedback on each small changes created and integrate it into future plan.
Cons: Teams build documentations throughout a project, which means they only have limited documentations in the beginning.
      Teams cannot predict cost, time and recourses required at the beginning.

(b)
A Scrum Master is a facilitator and coach for a team and support them to go through a project.
Their responsiblities are guiding ceremonies, removing blockers, and fostering collaboration.

(c)
1. Spring Planning: Held at the start of the cycle. The team defines the goal and select backlog they are going to work on.
2. Daily Scrum: A brief, daily check-in where members describe their own tasks to the team.
3. Sprint Review: Held at the end of the cycle. The team shows their work from the cycle to stakeholders and gets feedbacks.
